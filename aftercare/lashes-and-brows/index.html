Lash & Brow Aftercare

  • Keep lashes and brows dry for 24 hours after a lift, tint or lamination. No steam, saunas or swimming.
  • Avoid mascara and brow products for 24 hours.
  • For extensions, use oil-free products and brush them gently every day.
  • Don't pick or pull at extensions. Book a removal instead.

This is general advice. If you're worried about anything, get in touch or speak to a GP or pharmacist.
