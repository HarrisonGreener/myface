Filler Aftercare

  • Avoid touching or massaging the area for 24 hours unless you've been told to.
  • Skip make-up on the area for 12–24 hours.
  • Avoid alcohol, strenuous exercise, saunas and heat for 24–48 hours.
  • Swelling and bruising are normal and usually settle within a few days. A cold compress can help.
  • Your final result shows after around two weeks. Come back for your review appointment.
  • Get help straight away if you notice severe or worsening pain, skin that turns white, blue or blotchy, or any changes to your vision. Contact us immediately, and call 999 or go to A&E if you can't reach us.

This is general advice. If you're worried about anything, get in touch or speak to a GP or pharmacist.
