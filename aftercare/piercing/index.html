Piercing Aftercare

Piercing Aftercare

  • Clean twice a day with sterile saline spray. No alcohol, tea tree or harsh soaps.
  • Don't twist or turn the jewellery, and try not to touch it except when cleaning, with clean hands.
  • Avoid sleeping on new ear piercings. A travel pillow helps.
  • Keep it out of pools, hot tubs, the sea and baths until it's healed.
  • Some redness, swelling and clear or whitish fluid is normal early on.
  • Don't take the jewellery out to change it until it's fully healed.
  • If you see spreading redness, heat, yellow or green discharge, or feel unwell, get in touch and see a GP or pharmacist.

This is general advice. If you're worried about anything, get in touch or speak to a GP or pharmacist.
