Nail Aftercare

Nail Aftercare

  • Use cuticle oil daily to keep nails and skin healthy.
  • Wear gloves for cleaning and washing up.
  • Don't use your nails as tools for opening cans or scraping labels.
  • Never pick or peel off gel, BIAB or acrylics. Book a removal.
  • If a nail lifts or breaks, get in touch so we can fix it before water gets underneath.

This is general advice. If you're worried about anything, contact us or speak to a GP or pharmacist.
