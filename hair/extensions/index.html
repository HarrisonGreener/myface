FAQs

How long do tape-ins last?

Tape-ins are usually moved up every 6–8 weeks, and good quality hair can be reused for several refits.

Will they damage my hair?

Fitted and removed properly, and looked after at home, extensions shouldn't cause damage. Never remove them yourself.
