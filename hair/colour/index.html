FAQs

Do I need a patch test?

Yes. A skin patch test is needed at least 48 hours before any colour service, even if you've had colour before, because allergies can develop over time.

Can you take me from dark to blonde in one go?

Sometimes, but often it's safer done over a few sessions. You'll get an honest answer at your consultation about what's realistic for your hair.
