FAQs

Is filler permanent?

No. Hyaluronic acid fillers break down naturally over time.

Are there risks?

As with any injectable treatment, there are risks. Most side effects are mild, like swelling and bruising, and serious complications are rare. They'll be talked through with you before treatment.
