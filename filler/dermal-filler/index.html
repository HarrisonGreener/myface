Dermal Filler in Droylsden

About dermal filler

Dermal filler can add volume and contour to areas such as the chin, cheeks and jawline. Everything is planned at a consultation so results look like you.

Hyaluronic acid filler is temporary. How long it lasts depends on the area and product, which is explained at consultation.

FAQs

Is filler permanent?

No. Hyaluronic acid fillers break down naturally over time.

Are there risks?

As with any injectable treatment, there are risks. Most side effects are mild, like swelling and bruising, and serious complications are rare. They'll be talked through with you before treatment.
