Lip Filler in Droylsden

Lip filler adds volume, defines shape or balances the lips using a hyaluronic acid gel. Results can be kept subtle, and you can build up over more than one appointment.

Hyaluronic acid filler is temporary and gradually breaks down. Lip filler commonly lasts around 6 months, though it varies from person to person.

FAQs

How long does lip filler last?

Often around 6 months, but it varies depending on the product, the amount and your body.

Will I swell?

Some swelling and bruising is normal for a few days. It settles, and your final result shows after about two weeks.

Who can't have filler?

Anyone under 18, and anyone pregnant or breastfeeding. Some medical conditions and medications also rule it out, which gets checked at consultation.
